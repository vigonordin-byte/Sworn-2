You already know what you want to stop. The problem is the moment it arrives.

Sworn blocks the apps and sites you choose, during the hours you are most likely to give in. And when you go to switch that protection off, it does not simply let you.

SIXTY SECONDS, THEN A DECISION

Turning your protection off starts a timer you cannot skip. While it runs, Sworn shows you the reason you wrote on the day you committed, in your own words. At zero the decision is yours again, made by you rather than by the moment.

PROTECT THE HOURS YOU ALREADY KNOW ABOUT

Late at night. Home alone. After everyone else is asleep. Set a commitment for the window that is actually difficult, choose what it covers, and Sworn locks it on schedule using Apple's Screen Time. It runs whether the app is open or not.

ONE TAP WHEN IT COMES EARLY

Feeling it now? Press I'm tempted and everything under your commitments locks for an hour. No timer to sit through and nothing to explain.

A RECORD, NOT JUST A STREAK

Sworn shows how many days you actually kept, which hours are hardest for you, and how many urges you met and walked away from. The streak counts days you were genuinely protected, so if you switch everything off it stops climbing. A number that cannot be wrong is worth more than a number that only goes up.

BUILT AROUND WHAT YOU ARE WORKING ON

Choose it when you start, whether that is explicit content, gambling and betting, or endless scrolling, and Sworn adapts what it asks you and what it says back.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Apple's picker hands Sworn a token rather than a name, so it can count how many apps you protect and genuinely cannot tell which they are. What you write stays on your phone. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

Sworn is not therapy, medical care, or treatment for addiction. It adds friction to a moment where you would rather have some, and it keeps an honest record of how you did.
