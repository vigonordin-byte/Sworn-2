Support

Sworn is made by a very small team. If something is broken, or the app is not doing what you expected, tell us through the App Store's support link and a person will read it.

Include what you were doing and what happened. If it is about blocking, say which apps and which hours. That is almost always where the answer is.

Blocking is not working

Sworn blocks apps using Apple's Screen Time. Two things have to be true: you granted Screen Time access when asked, and the commitment has apps chosen and is switched on. You can check both under Commitments.

If you denied Screen Time access, iOS will not ask again. Turn it back on in Settings → Screen Time.

I want my data removed

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. That erases your record from this phone and from our server, lifts any blocking, and cannot be undone. Deleting the app alone removes the copy on your phone but leaves the account.
